How it works

  1. Talk

    We start with two questions: what are your goals, and what are your organization’s current goals? Then we decide which parts I can help with and make a plan.

  2. Clarify

    I email thoroughly or get on a call to understand what you want to accomplish. Then I translate it into the marketing materials you need.

  3. Review and launch

    I do the work and show it to you. You can review it as many times as you like, and nothing goes live until you say so. I only ask for your input when I need it, so you can stay focused on what you do best.

I reply within 24 hours, except on weekends and holidays.
