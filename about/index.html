Hi, I’m Anna.

I run Lucky Pixels Design from Pasadena, California.

My degree is in communication and journalism, so I’m trained in the written and spoken word. My career is in enterprise software, specifically helping big companies with their Master Data Management needs. I enjoy technically selling software and making the solution work for customers. I’ve been a presales engineer and a solutions architect. Both the writing and software skills translate into helping you define your marketing identity, design your website, and communicate your message so you can inform, educate, and serve your community.

What clients come to me with

They’re overwhelmed by their website. They spend hours trying to make changes, and it still doesn’t work.

They need someone to talk through the ideas sloshing around in their heads about positioning (where they fit) and differentiation (what makes them different). Talking it through, making a decision, and then seeing it translated onto the page brings huge clarity. Then they can execute on the strategy.

They don’t want to deal with the technical stuff. They want to focus on their job and do what they do best.
