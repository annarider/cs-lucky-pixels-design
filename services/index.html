Services

Start with one service or combine them. Not sure what you need? Start with a conversation.

Brand strategy

Before we choose colors or write a word, we decide who you serve and why they should choose you. Together we work out your positioning, your name, how you’re different from others who do similar work, your pricing, and clear answers to the “why you?” question. Then you walk away with finished materials, like your website and flyers, built on those decisions.

Copywriting

Words on a page, whether that’s a web page, an ad, a flyer, or printed material. I help you shape your message, in the tone and with the meaning you intend, so you can inform and educate the community you serve.

Visual identity

A logo, color palette, graphics, and fonts designed to help you stand out and help your community remember you, whenever they visit your website or see your marketing materials.

Web design

A complete website built around the information your community needs, focused on simplicity and ease of use. Your website works on any device (phone, tablet, or computer), so people can reach you while they’re on the go. After launch, I handle maintenance, updates, and improvements, and you get quick, responsive support. You never have to worry about the technical side: you outsource being a geek to me.

How pricing works

Choose a monthly subscription, which includes ongoing maintenance and updates, or a single fixed project price.
